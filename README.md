A single joke about the modern fitness trap: the same device that cheers your workout also knows exactly how close the snacks are. One line, one laugh — no setup beyond the watch on your wrist, and no punchline beyond the fridge.

> My smartwatch congratulated me on burning 400 calories, then reminded me the fridge is only twelve steps away.

Run `python3 main.py` to print the entry. The script reads `entry.txt` next to it and writes its contents to stdout byte-for-byte, with no extra formatting.
