This entry turns a fitness tracker into a brutally honest friend. It celebrates a workout in one breath and undercuts it in the next, because the reward for burning calories is apparently just around the corner. The joke lands on the gap between health goals and everyday temptation, and the smartwatch knows exactly how close the fridge is.

> My smartwatch congratulated me on burning 400 calories, then reminded me the fridge is only twelve steps away.

Run `main.py` and it prints the line from `entry.txt`.
